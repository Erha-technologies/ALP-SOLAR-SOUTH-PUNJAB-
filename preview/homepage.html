Best for: Reducing electricity bills

Panels feed power directly into your home or business alongside the grid connection, offsetting the units you'd otherwise buy from the utility.

Grid connection
Connected — exports excess generation via net metering where available.
Battery
No battery. Lowest-cost entry point into solar.
Backup
No backup during a grid outage — the inverter shuts off for safety.
Best for: Bill reduction and backup power

Combines grid-tied solar with battery storage, so surplus daytime generation is stored and used to carry essential loads through an outage.

Grid connection
Connected, with automatic switch-over to battery during loadshedding.
Battery
Battery sized to your chosen backup duration.
Backup
Seamless backup for the circuits you choose to protect.

FAQ

Common Questions

View All FAQs
How many solar panels do I need? ⌄

Depends on usage, roof area, and how much of your load you want to offset. Use the calculator for an instant estimate.

How much does a solar system cost? ⌄

Depends on system size, equipment tier, and site complexity — request a current price for your requirement.

What's the difference between hybrid and on-grid? ⌄

On-grid reduces your bill but shuts off during an outage. Hybrid adds battery storage for backup power too.

Do solar panels work during loadshedding? ⌄

Only with a hybrid or off-grid system with battery storage — a plain on-grid system shuts off for safety.
